# Lab 4 — Arthur J. Gallagher & Co. (NYSE: AJG)

## Investment view

Arthur J. Gallagher & Co. is an insurance brokerage, risk-management, and consulting company. Its brokerage operations place insurance and reinsurance for clients and earn commissions from underwriting enterprises or fees from clients; its risk-management operations provide claims management, loss-control, and consulting services. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 1, pp. 5-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/354190/000162828026008662/ajg-20251231.htm)

The investment question is whether returns from AJG's acquisition program will justify the associated debt, integration expense, and acquired-intangible amortization. I need more evidence that the AssuredPartners integration is generating durable organic growth and cash generation rather than solely a larger reported revenue base. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, pp. 35-67](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/354190/000162828026008662/ajg-20251231.htm)

## Operating evidence

Brokerage represented 87% of 2025 revenue. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 35](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/354190/000162828026008662/ajg-20251231.htm) This concentration makes the brokerage segment's client service, insurance placement, premium levels, and retention central to the investment case.

AJG reported $13.778 billion of revenue before reimbursements and $1.494 billion of net earnings attributable to controlling interests in FY2025. [2025 Form 10-K, Consolidated Statement of Earnings, p. 70](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/354190/000162828026008662/ajg-20251231.htm) The $164 million of reimbursements was offset by the same expense, so revenue before reimbursements is the more useful operating top-line measure. [2025 Form 10-K, Consolidated Statement of Earnings, p. 70](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/354190/000162828026008662/ajg-20251231.htm)

The company reported $1.122 billion of depreciation and amortization and $145 million of capital expenditures in FY2025. [2025 Form 10-K, Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows, p. 73](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/354190/000162828026008662/ajg-20251231.htm) I would separate recurring operating costs from integration costs, transaction costs, and acquired-intangible amortization before using earnings in a valuation model.

AJG's 2023 Form 10-K said that it had expanded, and expected to continue expanding, its international operations through acquisitions and organic growth. [AJG 2023 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 34](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/354190/000095017024013370/ajg-20231231.htm) In the 2025 Form 10-K, AJG said it expanded its international operations through acquisitions and organic growth during 2025. [2025 Form 10-K, Item 7, p. 35](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/354190/000162828026008662/ajg-20251231.htm)

## Acquisition and capital structure

AJG disclosed $15.766 billion of acquisition cash outflows in FY2025. [2025 Form 10-K, Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows, p. 73](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/354190/000162828026008662/ajg-20251231.htm) At December 31, 2025, it reported $13.099 billion of gross corporate and other debt and $1.396 billion of cash and cash equivalents. [2025 Form 10-K, Consolidated Balance Sheet, p. 72; Note 7, p. 94](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/354190/000162828026008662/ajg-20251231.htm) Higher revenue therefore does not by itself demonstrate that the acquisitions created value for common shareholders.

AJG's Q2 2026 release identifies acquisition-integration and transaction-related costs associated with AssuredPartners and Woodruff Sawyer. [Gallagher, Q2 2026 earnings release, July 30, 2026, pp. 7-10](https://investor.ajg.com/news/news-details/2026/Arthur-J--Gallagher--Co--Announces-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx) The same release states that AJG raised approximately $14 billion in cash through a follow-on common-stock offering and senior-notes issuance to fund AssuredPartners, and identifies successful integration, unrealized expected benefits, debt levels, and insurance-market conditions among material forward-looking risks. [Gallagher, Q2 2026 earnings release, July 30, 2026, pp. 7-10](https://investor.ajg.com/news/news-details/2026/Arthur-J--Gallagher--Co--Announces-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx)
